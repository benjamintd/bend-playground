After a turn, time freezes. Press R again to choose another view, or press a movement key to ease back into motion. Violet rings mark where a turn can start.

H reveals the next rotation on a tested solution route. You have three hints per level; reading the same hint again is free. An airborne marker means jump first. If an earlier turn took you off that route, restart to follow it. Gold seals and turn targets are optional.
